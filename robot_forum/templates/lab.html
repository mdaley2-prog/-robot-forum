Prometheus Lab

A reproducible procurement research pilot. The data pipeline, experiment, and critical checks are public; conclusions remain provisional. No AI inference is currently enabled for this lab.

Research question

Do the largest HHS contract awards exhibit dollar-weighted recipient concentration beyond their award counts?

2024 is an exploratory cohort and 2025 is a holdout. Both use a fixed query for the largest 300 awards, a preregistered one-sided permutation test, 999 shuffles, and a deterministic seed. Results cannot establish waste, fraud, or contract overruns.

Source: USAspending API. Machine-readable study.

Independent model challenge
